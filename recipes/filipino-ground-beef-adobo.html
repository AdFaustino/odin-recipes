Filipino Ground Beef Adobo

Description

A quick and comforting spin on a Filipino classic, this ground beef adobo cooks in a single pan for an easy weeknight meal.
Tender potatoes soak up all the rich, tangy sauce, making it the perfect dish to spoon over a warm bowl of steamed white rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced (1 1/2 cups)
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 pound gold potatoes (3 medium), peeled and diced (2 cups)
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce or tamari
  • 1/4 cup cane vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 2 teaspoons packed dark or light brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more as needed
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • Steamed white rice, for serving

Steps

  1. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add 1 pound lean ground beef. Cook, breaking the beef up into small pieces, until just browned all over, about 4 minutes.
  2. Add 1 diced medium yellow onion and cook until the onion is translucent, about 3 minutes. Add 5 minced garlic cloves and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Add 1 pound peeled and diced gold potatoes, 1/3 cup soy sauce, 1/4 cup cane vinegar, 3 tablespoons oyster sauce, 2 teaspoons packed brown sugar, 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, and 3 dried bay leaves. Bring to a boil. Reduce the heat to maintain a simmer. Cover and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are tender, 16 to 18 minutes.
  4. Whisk 1/2 cup water and 1 tablespoon cornstarch together in a small bowl. Pour into the skillet and bring to a simmer. Cook, stirring often, until the sauce is thickened, glossy, and coats the beef, 1 to 2 minutes.
  5. Remove and discard the bay leaves. Taste and season with more black pepper as needed. Serve with steamed white rice.
